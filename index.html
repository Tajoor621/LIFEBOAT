Prepare before device loss

Keep independent copies of important information, protect account recovery methods, and verify that backups can actually be opened. LIFEBOAT features are limited to the sources and permissions currently implemented in the Android app.

Know the limits

If a device is lost and no independent backup exists, local-only data may be unrecoverable. LIFEBOAT does not bypass locks, encryption, account security, FRP, Activation Lock or device management. The web shell does not upload or collect personal backup data.
